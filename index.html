Improve your control

Quick headshot calibration

Don't copy settings blindly. Make small changes and test them in Training Mode.

Set a baseline

Choose one preset close to your device and keep your HUD and DPI settings consistent during testing.

Test short drags

Practice on stationary targets first. Use a comfortable upward movement rather than forcing a very long drag.

Adjust gradually

If your aim overshoots, lower General or Red Dot slightly. If it stops short, raise one setting a little and retest.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are these settings guaranteed headshots?

No. Sensitivity is personal and depends on device, touch response, FPS, HUD and practice. Use presets as a starting point only.

How do I find my best sensitivity?

Start with a preset, test it in Training Mode and change one value at a time in small increments.
